Welcome

How does
Book Mode work?

One Habit at a Time takes a different approach to weight loss: instead of overhauling everything on day one, you install a single habit — cue, routine, reward — until it runs on autopilot, then move to the next. Book Mode turns that exact method into a daily practice inside the app.

You still get a wing in the background — the same check-ups and meal photos regular Wingpact users rely on, plus up to 2 friends you can invite for free — but the app's main job here is simpler: help you install one habit at a time, exactly like the book teaches.

01 · redeem & onboarding

Unlock your

Redeem Code

The code printed in your copy of One Habit at a Time turns on Book Mode for your Wingpact account. No separate app, no separate login.

1
Create your account

Sign up with email or Google, same as any Wingpact user. Already have an account? Skip straight to step 2.

2
Enter your book code

New sign-ups see a dashed "Got a code from the book?" callout right at the top of onboarding. Existing users find it in Settings → "I have a code from the book." Type the code exactly as printed.

3
Set up your profile — in pounds & feet

Book Mode's onboarding asks for height in feet/inches and weight in pounds throughout, matching how the book itself talks about your body. Nothing to convert yourself.

4
Meet Habit One

Your first habit — Water Before Food — unlocks immediately. It's free for as long as you need it, no trial clock counting down.

Tip · Already a Wingpact user?

Redeeming a code from an existing account just adds book access to it — same login, same data, nothing to duplicate. Your regular subscription (if you have one) is unaffected.

Dashboard

The Home screen is the first thing you see every time you open the app. It shows today's calories, your weight chart in pounds, and quick shortcuts to your most important actions.

This Guide & SOS

The guide banner links to this document. The SOS button sends an immediate support alert to your wing.

Book Mode replaces the social feed and challenges tabs with a dedicated Habits tab (p. 9) — the app's job here is helping you install one habit, not run a social feed. Nothing is deleted from your account, it's just not shown.

Meals · AI Camera

Photograph every meal — Claude AI analyzes calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds. Not accurate? Add a text hint and get a fresh analysis. No camera? Use the Manual button.

4
Check the analysis

5

6

Daily Check-up

The check-up is 2 minutes that wrap up your day. After saving, AI generates a personal summary grounded in your current habit, with a tip for tomorrow.

Water Intake

Drag the slider or tap a quick button (0.5L – 4L). Goal: 2–2.5 liters — the same habit as p. 9's Habit One.

Weight — in pounds

Log today's weight in lb. The dashboard chart and your profile both stay in pounds automatically.

Workout

Toggle on, choose type + intensity + duration. AI calculates calories burned.

Mood

Choose from 5 emoji. Mood is saved in history and visible to wing members.

AI Daily Summary

After tapping "Save" — tap "Close Day" to receive a personal AI summary grounded in the habit you're currently working on: what you did well, and a specific tip for tomorrow, not generic advice.

05 · calendar

Your history,
day by day

Calendar

The calendar brings every check-up together in one month view — a dot marks any day you logged data. Tap a day to see the full breakdown. Open it from the hamburger menu (☰) → Calendar.

1
Navigate between months

The arrows on either side of the month name move one month back/forward. You can't advance into a future month.

2
Dots mean logged data

Any day with a check-up gets a small dot under the date. No dot — nothing was logged.

3
Tap a day for detail

A panel opens at the bottom with everything logged that day: water, weight, steps, workout, mood and notes.

4
Swipe between days

Inside the panel, swipe left/right to move to the previous/next day without going back to the grid.

Tip · Retroactive Check-up

Forgot to log yesterday? Open that day from the calendar and fill in the check-up after the fact — so a one-off slip doesn't cost you your streak.

BMI · TDEE Calculator

The in-app calculator (Menu → Calculator) computes your BMI, Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), and a tailored calorie target — and saves them straight to your profile. In Book Mode, height and weight are entered in feet/inches and pounds.

1
Pick a gender

Feeds the BMR formula, which differs statistically between men and women.

2
Enter age, height, weight and target weight

Height in feet/inches, weight in pounds — four simple fields.

3
Choose an activity level

Five levels — sedentary through very active. The higher the level, the higher your TDEE (and the calorie target derived from it).

4
Tap "Calculate"

Four results appear instantly: BMI, BMR, TDEE, and a daily target tailored for weight loss.

5
"Save to Profile"

Updates your daily calorie target across the whole app — dashboard, check-up, and everywhere progress is shown against a goal.

Tip · Recalculate Periodically

Your weight changes — your calorie target should change with it. Come back to the calculator every 3–4 weeks and update your current weight so your daily target stays accurate.

07 · your habits

One habit.
One at a time.

Habits

The Habits tab is Book Mode's home base. Instead of a social feed, it shows the one habit you're working on right now, straight from the book: its cue, its routine, its reward, and your own trigger sentence.

1
Your current habit card

Name, cue → routine → reward, and the exact trigger sentence from the book for this habit — e.g. Habit One's is "Before I eat anything, I drink a full glass of water."

2
"What you're probably thinking"

The book's own answers to common doubts about this specific habit, so hesitation gets addressed before it stalls you.

3
Hard situations

The book's guidance for exactly the situations where this habit is hardest to keep — restaurants, travel, drinking, and more, depending on the habit.

4
Mark it installed

When the habit feels automatic — the book's own benchmark is a median of 66 days, typically 18–254 — tap "Mark this habit installed." The next habit unlocks immediately.

5
All 8 installed

Once all 8 are installed, the Habits tab becomes your track record — every habit you've made automatic, in order.

Tip · No Rush

There's no clock here. Habit One stays free and available for as long as you need — Book Mode only asks you to subscribe once you've marked it installed and move on to Habit Two.

FAQ + Tips
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I redeem my code from the book?
Settings → "I have a code from the book" (or the callout at the top of sign-up) → enter the code exactly as printed. It unlocks Book Mode on your account immediately.
Do I have to finish Habit One by a deadline?
No — Habit One has no day limit. Take as long as the book recommends (median 66 days, range 18–254) before marking it installed.
Can my invited friends see my check-ups?
Yes — inviting friends adds them to your wing (up to 20 members total), same as regular Wingpact. Up to 2 of them ride on your subscription for free.
What happens after I mark a habit installed?
The next habit unlocks immediately on the Habits tab — its cue, routine, reward and trigger sentence, plus the book's own answers to common doubts about it.
Does Book Mode use pounds or kilograms?
Pounds and feet/inches throughout — profile, check-up, dashboard chart — matching how the book itself talks about your body. Nothing to convert yourself.
I'm not in Book Mode but bought the book — can I switch?
Yes. From an existing Wingpact account, go to Settings → "I have a code from the book" and redeem your code — book access is added to your current account.
7 Tips for Your First Week
01

Snap everything — even coffee. Snacks, caloric drinks, sweets. A 3-second AI analysis is worth far more than "I'll log it later."

02

Check-up = routine. Pick a fixed time — 9 PM for example. The notification will remind you.

03

Say your trigger sentence out loud. Habit One's is "Before I eat anything, I drink a full glass of water." Saying it in the moment beats trying to remember it.

04

Read the AI summary. Not to feel bad, but to learn. It's grounded in the habit you're actually working on.

05

Don't wait for the "perfect" day. A 2,400-calorie day that's logged beats a "clean" day that isn't tracked at all.

06

There's no rush. Don't mark a habit installed just to see the next one. "Installed" means automatic, not "done for today" — the book's own range is 18–254 days.

07

Steps = everything. Health Connect (Google Fit / Samsung Health) syncs automatically. 8,000 steps a day = ~260 extra calories burned — no special workout required.

Android App

Wingpact is also a full Android app — with automatic step counting, a home-screen widget, and smart notifications. Everything stays in sync with the web version, and none of it changes in Book Mode.

Automatic step counting
1
Connect to Health Connect

On first launch the app asks permission to read your steps. Approve once — that's it.

2
Automatic sync

Steps are read from Health Connect (Google Fit / Samsung Health) and appear on your dashboard — no manual entry.

3
Manual editing always available

No Health Connect? You can edit steps manually in the Quick Log card on the dashboard.

Push notifications
1
Someone encouraged you

A comment or encouragement on your meal or check-up triggers an instant notification.

2
SOS alert

A member tapped "needs support" — the whole wing is notified. Tapping it opens their page.

3
5 PM reminder

Haven't logged a check-up by the afternoon? A gentle nudge so you don't break your streak.
