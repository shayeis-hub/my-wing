Welcome

How does
Wingpact work?

Migratory birds fly in a V-formation for one simple reason: when the leading bird breaks the wind, every member of the flock expends less energy. When the leader tires, another bird takes their place. That's how they travel distances no single bird could manage alone.

That's exactly the Wingpact model: a group of up to 20 people who share meals, do a daily check-up, and compete in friendly challenges together. Not competition — collaboration.

Photo & Enjoy

Photograph your plate — Claude AI analyzes calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds. Your wing sees what you ate.

Daily Check-up

2 minutes each evening: water, veggies, workout, eating window and mood. AI summarizes your day and adds a tip for tomorrow.

Social Feed

Share posts, answer the group's daily question, and react with quick emoji reactions. The wing keeps each other motivated.

Meals · AI Camera

Photograph every meal — Claude AI analyzes calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds. Not accurate? Add a text hint and get a fresh analysis. No camera? Use the Manual button.

Step 1 · Photo
1
Tap "Photo"

From the Meals screen tap the Photo button at the top left to open the camera or choose from gallery. You can attach several photos to one meal.

2
Frame your plate

The scan line moves across the image — food items are identified one by one.

3
AI analyzes

Calories, protein, carbs and fat appear instantly.

Step 2 · Review & Save
4
Check the analysis

See the full breakdown. Tap "Correct" to edit values manually.

5
Add a hint (optional)

"No tahini" / "That was whole-grain bread" — AI re-analyzes with your correction.

6
Save to Wing

Tap "Save" — the meal appears in the wing's shared feed instantly.

Tip · Manual & Retroactive Logging

No camera available? Tap "Manual" to add a meal with a text description. Forgot to log yesterday? You can add a meal for a past date — tap the date icon in the form and pick any day.

03 · daily check-up

Your evening
ritual

Daily Check-up

The check-up is 2 minutes that wrap up your day. After saving, AI generates a personal summary with insights and a tip for tomorrow. Your wing can cheer you on your check-up.

Water Intake

Drag the slider or tap a quick button (0.5L – 4L). Goal: 2–2.5 liters.

Vegetables

How many servings of vegetables today? Tap +/− to count.

Workout

Toggle on, choose type + intensity + duration. AI calculates calories burned.

Mood

Choose from 5 emoji. Mood is saved in history and visible to wing members.

Eating Window Recommended 8–10 hrs

The app auto-detects your first and last meal. Tap "Edit manually" to adjust. Duration, Open (first meal) and Close (last meal) are shown at a glance.

AI Daily Summary

After tapping "Save" — tap "Close Day" to receive a personal AI summary: analysis of what you did well, 2–3 insights, and a specific tip for tomorrow. The summary is saved in the calendar.

04 · calendar

Your history,
day by day

Calendar

The calendar brings every check-up together in one month view — a dot marks any day you logged data. Tap a day to see the full breakdown. Open it from the hamburger menu (☰) → Calendar.

1
Navigate between months

The arrows on either side of the month name move one month back/forward. You can't advance into a future month.

2
Dots mean logged data

Any day with a check-up (yours or another wing member's) gets a small dot under the date. No dot — nothing was logged.

3
Tap a day for detail

A panel opens at the bottom with everything logged that day: water, veggies, steps, workout, mood and notes — yours and your wing members'.

4
Swipe between days

Inside the panel, swipe left/right to move to the previous/next day without going back to the grid.

5
Encourage right from the panel

Spot an old check-up from a wing member? You can send encouragement directly from the panel, no need to leave the calendar.

Tip · Retroactive Check-up

Forgot to log yesterday? Open that day from the calendar and fill in the check-up after the fact — so a one-off slip doesn't cost you your streak.

BMI · TDEE Calculator

The in-app calculator (Menu → Calculator) computes your BMI, Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), and a tailored calorie target — and saves them straight to your profile.

1
Pick a gender

Feeds the BMR formula, which differs statistically between men and women.

2
Enter age, height, weight and target weight

Four simple number fields.

3
Choose an activity level

Five levels — sedentary through very active. The higher the level, the higher your TDEE (and the calorie target derived from it).

4
Tap "Calculate"

Four results appear instantly: BMI, BMR, TDEE, and a daily target tailored for weight loss. Tap any result's (ⓘ) to see a short explanation of what it means.

5
"Save to Profile"

Updates your daily calorie target across the whole app — dashboard, check-up, and everywhere progress is shown against a goal.

Tip · Recalculate Periodically

Your weight changes — your calorie target should change with it. Come back to the calculator every 3–4 weeks and update your current weight so your daily target stays accurate.

06 · social feed

Share.
Encourage. Connect.

Feed · Daily Question

The Feed is the social heart of your wing — where members connect, share, and motivate each other. Every day opens with a Daily Question that sparks conversation, alongside free-form posts with text or photos.

Daily Question

A new question appears at the top of the feed each morning. Answer in text — all wing members see it. Tap ❤️/🔥/✨/🏆 to react to a teammate's answer.

Free Post

Tap the text field at the top of the feed → type text or attach a photo → share. Your post appears instantly for every wing member.

Quick Reactions

On every post and every meal in the feed — tap the reaction icon → choose ❤️ heart / 🔥 fire / ✨ sparkle / 🏆 trophy. The sender sees it instantly.

Text Comments

Tap the chat bubble on any post → type a comment → send. The comment count is shown on the post and on meals in the feed.

How the Daily Question works
1

A new question is generated automatically every day — about food, motivation, routines, and goals.

2

Each member can answer in text — all other wing members see every answer below the question.

3

React to teammates' answers with quick emoji reactions — encouragement in 2 seconds.

Tip · When to use the Feed

Morning — answer the daily question to start the day connected. After a workout — share a post with a photo. When a teammate posts a meal — tap a heart in seconds — it's enough, and it keeps their motivation alive.

FAQ + Tips
Frequently Asked Questions
What is BMR and why is it my calorie target?
BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body burns at rest. The TDEE/BMR calculator in the menu computes it from your age, height, weight, and activity level. That becomes your daily calorie goal.
Can my wing members see everything I do?
Yes — every meal photo, daily check-up, and step count. That's intentional! Part of the power is shared accountability. If you don't photograph a meal, your wing won't see it.
What happens when my streak breaks?
If you miss a day without checking in, your streak resets. You can do a retroactive check-up (for yesterday) via the calendar → select day → Edit.
How many people can be in a wing?
Up to 20 members (Premium). The free trial allows up to 3. A larger group creates broader accountability while keeping it personal enough to matter.
What's the difference between the trial and Premium?
The first 21 days are a free trial with full access to everything. After that, a Premium subscription unlocks unlimited meal analysis, challenges, AI summaries and more. You can continue in view-only mode without a subscription.
How do I add a new member to my wing?
Tap "Challenges" in the nav → Settings → "Copy Invite Link" → send the link to your friend. Tapping the link joins them automatically.
7 Tips for Your First Week
01

Snap everything — even coffee. Snacks, caloric drinks, sweets. A 3-second AI analysis is worth far more than "I'll log it later."

02

Check-up = routine. Pick a fixed time — 9 PM for example. The notification will remind you.

03

React to your wing. 3 seconds of encouragement after a healthy meal = motivates everyone. The meal feed is a social feed.

04

Read the AI summary. Not to feel bad, but to learn. The "tomorrow tip" is the most useful part.

05

Don't wait for the "perfect" day. A 2,400-calorie day that's logged beats a "clean" day that isn't tracked at all.

06

Launch a weekly challenge. An active challenge is a motivation engine for the whole group. Even "6,000 steps a day" keeps everyone moving.

07

Steps = everything. Health Connect (Google Fit / Samsung Health) syncs automatically. 8,000 steps a day = ~260 extra calories burned — no special workout required.

Business · Coach

A dietitian or coach who wants to guide clients gets a dashboard that's completely separate from the personal experience — no personal check-ups, just a client list. Find it at: Menu ← Profile ← "Client management" (crown icon). Note: signing up with Google always creates a personal account — use the email + password form to sign up as business.

1
Sign up as a business account

The sign-up screen has an "Account type" toggle: Personal vs Business / Coach. Choosing "Business" skips the personal height/weight/goals questionnaire and goes straight to plan selection.

2
Pick a plan

A 30-day free trial with one client, or one of the three paid tiers below. Upgrade or cancel any time from the dashboard.

3
Private client or group

"Private client" — single-use 1:1 link, opens a private two-person wing. "Group" — reusable link, behaves like a normal wing where everyone sees each other.

4
Track every client

Tapping a client opens their daily check-up, recent meals, and a private message wall between just the two of you.
