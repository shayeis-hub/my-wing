Welcome

How does
Wingpact work?

Migratory birds fly in a V-formation for one simple reason: when the leading bird breaks the wind, every member of the flock expends less energy. When the leader tires, another bird takes their place. That's how they travel distances no single bird could manage alone.

That's exactly the Wingpact model: a group of up to 3 people who share meals, do a daily check-in, and compete in friendly challenges together. Not competition — collaboration.

Snap & Eat

Photograph your plate — Claude AI analyzes calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds. Your wing sees what you ate.

Daily Check-in

2 minutes each evening: water, veggies, workout, eating window and mood. AI summarizes your day and adds a tip for tomorrow.

Challenges & Trophies

The wing leader launches weekly challenges — steps, water, veggies and more. Winners earn trophies displayed on their profile forever.

Dashboard

The dashboard is the main screen you see every time you open the app. It summarizes what you've eaten, what your wing is doing, and what your next action should be.

1
Calorie Card

The yellow-coral card at the top shows how many calories you've eaten against your BMR target. The dark bar shows your progress.

2
Three Key Stats

Below the card — steps, water, and streak (number of consecutive days you've checked in). Your streak is shown in coral.

3
Wing Today

Avatars show who's been active today. Tap a member's avatar to view their dashboard — meals, steps and check-in.

4
"Snap Meal" Button

The large yellow button is your main action. Tap it before every meal. Daily check-in is also accessible from the bottom nav.

Tip · Quick Nav

Tap any member's avatar to jump to their dashboard and see their meals, steps, and last check-in.

Meals · AI Camera

Photograph every meal — Claude AI analyzes calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds. Not accurate? Add a text hint and get a fresh analysis.

Step 1 · Snap
1
Tap "Snap Meal"

From the dashboard or tap Meals in the bottom nav, then the snap button.

2
Frame your plate

The yellow frame guides you. Wait until the plate is in focus, then tap the shutter.

3
AI analyzes

The yellow scan line moves across the image — food items are identified one by one.

Step 2 · Review & Save
4
Check the analysis

See calories, protein, carbs and fat. Not right? Tap "Correct".

5
Add a hint (optional)

"No tahini" / "That was whole-grain bread" — AI re-analyzes with your correction.

6
Save to Wing

Tap "Save" — the meal appears in every wing member's feed instantly.

Tip · Manual & Retroactive Logging

No camera available? Tap "Manual" to add a meal with a text description. Forgot to log yesterday? You can add a meal for a past date — tap the date icon in the form and pick any day.

Daily Check-in

The check-in is 2 minutes that wrap up your day. After saving, AI generates a personal summary with insights and a tip for tomorrow. Your wing can cheer you on your check-in.

Water

Drag the slider to the amount of water you drank. Goal: 2–2.5 liters.

Veggies

How many meals included vegetables? Tap +/− to count.

Workout

Toggle on, choose type + intensity + duration. AI calculates calories burned.

Mood

Choose from 5 emoji. Mood is saved in history and visible to wing members.

Eating Window Recommended 8–10 hrs

Set the time of your first and last meal. A narrower eating window supports better sleep quality and steady energy throughout the day.

AI Daily Summary

After tapping "Save" — tap "Close Day" to receive a personal AI summary: analysis of what you did well, 2–3 insights, and a specific tip for tomorrow. The summary is saved in the calendar.

FAQ + Tips
Frequently Asked Questions
What is BMR and why is it my calorie target?
BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body burns at rest. The TDEE/BMR calculator in the menu computes it from your age, height, weight, and activity level. That becomes your daily calorie goal.
Can my wing members see everything I do?
Yes — every meal photo, daily check-in, and step count. That's intentional! Part of the power is shared accountability. If you don't photograph a meal, your wing won't see it.
What happens when my streak breaks?
If you miss a day without checking in, your streak resets. You can do a retroactive check-in (for yesterday) via the calendar → select day → Edit.
How many people can be in a wing?
Up to 3 members. The small size is intentional — a small group creates real accountability. In a group of 20 it's easy to disappear.
What's the difference between the trial and Premium?
The first 7 days are a free trial with full access to everything. After that, a Premium subscription unlocks unlimited meal analysis, challenges, AI summaries and more. You can continue in view-only mode without a subscription.
How do I add a new member to my wing?
Tap "Challenges" in the nav → Settings → "Copy Invite Link" → send the link to your friend. Tapping the link joins them automatically.
7 Tips for Your First Week
01

Snap everything — even coffee. Snacks, caloric drinks, sweets. A 3-second AI analysis is worth far more than "I'll log it later."

02

Check-in = routine. Pick a fixed time — 9 PM for example. The notification will remind you.

03

React to your wing. 3 seconds of encouragement after a healthy meal = motivates everyone. The meal feed is a social feed.

04

Read the AI summary. Not to feel bad, but to learn. The "tomorrow tip" is the most useful part.

05

Don't wait for the "perfect" day. A 2,400-calorie day that's logged beats a "clean" day that isn't tracked at all.

06

Launch a weekly challenge. An active challenge is a motivation engine for the whole group. Even "7,000 steps a day" keeps everyone moving.

07

Steps = everything. Google Fit syncs automatically. 10,000 steps a day = ~350 extra calories burned — no special workout required.
